Needs-Driven Agendas

Agents don't just move randomly. Built on a Maslowian behavioral framework, daily schedules emerge dynamically based on needs for food, recreation, social interaction, and mandatory obligations like work or school.

Multi-Modal Kinematics

Agendas are translated into high-fidelity trajectories using the Valhalla routing engine. Agents seamlessly navigate OpenStreetMap infrastructure across 9 counties utilizing walking, biking, driving, bus, and rail modes based on GTFS data.

Synthesized Population Demographics

The simulated population is procedurally generated to reflect realistic urban distributions, accurately categorized by behavior, occupation, and vehicle access.
